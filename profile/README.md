Finding Care is hard. Paying for it is harder. And the Practitioners doing the
work are buried in tools that do not talk to each other.

Heyrafiki connects People, Practitioners, Insurers and Organizations through one
private layer for Care, Conversations, Benefits and Payments. Independent
Practitioners become insurance-eligible under a single Virtual Facility, and
they are paid the moment a Session ends.

### What we build in the open

The rails, so anyone can inspect them, extend them and hold us to them: client
SDKs, adapters for the healthcare standards health systems already run, the API
contract, the developer documentation, and the scoring for published screening
instruments with a citation for every one.

### What we keep closed, and why

Safety and triage thresholds, anti-fraud logic, the Practitioner verification
pipeline, and integrations bound by a payer contract. Publishing those would
arm the people we are protecting others from. The full reasoning is at
[heyrafiki.space/open-source](https://heyrafiki.space/open-source).

### Working with us

Issues and pull requests are welcome on every public repository. Start with
`CONTRIBUTING.md`. Report anything security-related through `SECURITY.md`
rather than a public issue: this code sits near health data, and we would
rather hear from you first.
